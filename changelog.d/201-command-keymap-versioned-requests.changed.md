`CommandKeymapPreview`, `CommandKeymapCommit`, and `CommandKeymapReset` now carry `protocol_version` (`CommandKeymapProtocolVersion::CURRENT` in Rust and `protocolVersion: COMMAND_KEYMAP_PROTOCOL_VERSION` in TypeScript), and `CommandKeymapProtocolVersion` rejects unsupported versions during deserialization. Migration: pass `protocol_version: CommandKeymapProtocolVersion::CURRENT` (Rust) or `protocolVersion: COMMAND_KEYMAP_PROTOCOL_VERSION` (TypeScript) when constructing keymap preview, commit, or reset requests.
