`ForkHistory::record_applied` now derives encoded weight from `HistoryPolicy` (removing caller-supplied `encoded_weight` from `ForkRecord` and adding `ForkHistoryError::Policy`), `ForkLoadError` splits `PayloadMigration` from `Payload`, and fork pruning and branch-page projection are bounded without full-lineage materialization. `NotificationLedger::publish_once` remembers producer tokens across dismissal and pruning in a bounded ring (`NotificationPublishOutcome::AlreadyPublished` now carries `notification_id` and `record: Option<NotificationRecord>`), `NotificationLedger::dismiss` takes `NotificationDismiss`, no-op `change_retention` preserves the revision, and `observe_terminal_operation` accepts `OperationTerminalEvidence` for transitions, confirmed queued cancellations, and teardown. `OperationCatalogue::request_cancellation` now transitions queued work to `Cancelling` for executor dispatch unless `QueuedCancellationConfirmation::ConfirmedUnstarted` is supplied, and `retry_of` no longer requires the source operation to remain retained.
