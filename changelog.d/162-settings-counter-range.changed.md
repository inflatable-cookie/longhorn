**Settings counters are bounded to the JavaScript number range.** `SettingsRegistryGeneration` and `SettingsScopeRevision` wrap `longhorn_core::WireCounter`: deserializing a snapshot or command whose `generation`, `scopeRevision` or `registryGeneration` exceeds `2^53 - 1` now fails instead of producing a value the TypeScript validator rejects, `SettingsRegistryGeneration::new` and `SettingsScopeRevision::new` panic above `WireCounter::MAX`, and advancing either reports overflow there rather than at `u64::MAX`. Migration: keep any larger counter in host-owned state that never crosses the wire, construct with deserialization where the input is external, and do not catch `ScopeRevisionOverflow` as a `u64`-end case.
