Native-content connections share the snapshot connection lifecycle: an invalid request no longer leaks its pending slot, and `dispose()` finishes cleanup before it rejects with an unlisten failure. A start failure, already in `failures()`, still does not reject `dispose()`. Shared `CheckedSnapshotConnection` gains `offer()` for snapshots returned by a caller's own request. A throwing consumer callback in `listenTauriBridgeJob` no longer ends the job or reports a malformed event; a malformed progress event during terminal registration releases the terminal listener. The Svelte operation panel closes its cancel dialog when the request fails, and the failure shows on the page. Window drag ignores tab, link, menu-item, label, summary and editable targets. Settings shell apply, guard and reset failures show on the page instead of being discarded, and config pages show a restore refusal's detail rather than its protocol code, with archive timestamps localised. Settings scope connections record accepted snapshots after acceptance rather than inside the predicate. The agent-control shim bundle is regenerated for its source changes.
