`longhorn-pty` now carries the numeric `raw_os_error` on every OS-backed `Failure` variant (with a `Failure::raw_os_error()` accessor and `(os error <code>)` in `Display`), sets `CLOEXEC` atomically on slave opens (`OFlags::CLOEXEC`), descriptor duplications (`fcntl_dupfd_cloexec`), and Linux/FreeBSD master opens (`OpenptFlags::CLOEXEC`), documents the shared non-blocking master file description, and wakes an output reader blocked on a full queue when `close()` runs so PTY descriptors are released even if the consumer stops reading. Pre-1.0 breaking: OS-backed `Failure` variants are now struct variants carrying `raw_os_error`. Contract 028 is updated to match.
