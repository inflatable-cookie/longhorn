`DispatchBounds::new` no longer accepts `max_progress_item_bytes` or `max_queued_progress_items`, and those accessors have been removed from `longhorn-agent-tool-dispatch` because the dispatcher enforces only the five bounds it owns (`max_calls_per_binding`, `max_outstanding_calls`, `max_argument_bytes`, `max_result_bytes`, and `max_call_duration`); remove those two arguments from `DispatchBounds::new` calls and enforce progress limits at your own progress boundary.
