Apply commands require the observed staged digest, and superseded or cancelled transfers cannot stage bytes. `PreparedTransfer::new` now allocates a transfer identity and can no longer be called in a const context. `StagedArtifact::new` derives its version from the verified artifact. Child-view sources require HTTPS or loopback HTTP. Isolated-window requests refused at capacity can retry with the same ID. Replay history and teardown reports are bounded.
