Settings registry generations and scope revisions are now wire counters, so a host can no longer stamp a generation or revision above `2^53 - 1` that its own TypeScript client refuses as an unsafe integer. Breaking: `SettingsRegistryGeneration::new` and `SettingsScopeRevision::new` panic above `WireCounter::MAX`, deserialization rejects such a value, `SettingsRegistryGeneration::checked_next` returns `Result<Self, WireCounterOverflow>` instead of `Option<Self>`, and revision advance now overflows at `WireCounter::MAX` rather than `u64::MAX`. Migrate a caller that needs the old range by keeping its own counter; the value cannot cross the wire either way. `SettingsLimits` rejects unknown fields on the wire, so a snapshot's limits object accepts the twelve fields TypeScript already requires and nothing else. Seal now rejects duplicate page search keywords after case folding with `DuplicateItem` instead of dropping them, and reports `InvalidPageFeatures` rather than `MissingReference` when a page offers reset without a reset-capable writable apply unit.
