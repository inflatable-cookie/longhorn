Unify `SurfaceDocument` validation, limits, and configuration persistence under one validator (`validate_document` / `normalize_document` / `validate_normalized_document` / `validate_normalized_layout`), one limits type (`SurfaceLimits` with `SurfaceLimits::builder()`, replacing `LayoutLimits`), and one `longhorn-surfaces-config` stack (`RegisteredSurfaceDomain`, `PersistedSurfaceDocument`, `SurfaceMigration`, `LegacySurfaceFormatMigration`, `SurfaceBackupPolicy`, removing `RegisteredLayoutDomain` and its sibling duplicates). Consumer-visible breaks: `CloseSurface` now removes the closed Surface's regions, sizing slots, and placed panel instances in the same commit; `DuplicateSurface` preserves `SurfacePresentation`; `MoveSurface` active-Surface fallback uses primary-member index; `validate_document`, `normalize_document`, `validate_normalized_document`, `validate_normalized_layout`, `resolve_surfaces`, `compose_surface_window_plan`, `SurfaceMutationEngine::new`, and `RegisteredSurfaceDomain::new` bind `&LayoutDefinitionRegistry` (with `PersistedSurfaceDocument` carrying `registryDigest`, so consumers upgrading pre-unification digest-less Surface or prior-digest layout files bump their `DomainDescriptor` schema version, attach `LegacySurfaceFormatMigration` or a custom `SurfaceMigration`, and rewrite through `ConfigStore::rewrite_migrated_domain` with a pre-migration safety backup); `debounced_layout_presentation` binds presentation debounce lanes directly to their `RegisteredSurfaceDomain`'s registry (replacing `LayoutPresentationStrategy::new`); `publish_surface_mutation` and `commit_surface_transfer` drop their per-call registry argument while `admit_panel_session` and `commit_panel_transfer` take `&RegisteredSurfaceDomain<M>`; and `PanelInstancePolicy::OnePerContainer` is renamed to `PanelInstancePolicy::OnePerSurface` (`"one_per_surface"` on the wire) across Rust, generated TypeScript bindings, and `@inflatable-cookie/longhorn-poodle-svelte`.
