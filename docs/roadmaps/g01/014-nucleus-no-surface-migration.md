## Outcome

Make Nucleus the first simple workspace consumer and prove the full layout
stack does not require Surfaces.

## Generation Runway Goal

Prove one real Surface-free consumer before Loophole adopts the full optional
hierarchy. Transfer shared mechanism authority without moving Nucleus project,
task, runtime, resource, panel-catalogue, or browser policy into Longhorn.
