## Acceptance Criteria

- every Surface has exactly one habitat and one valid layout binding where required
- focused promotion is adjacent, singleton, fallback-retaining, and atomic
- close returns the panel without stale claims or resurrection

- native fullscreen changes no durable Surface or workspace field

- Pulse restore truth and local fallback become competing authorities
