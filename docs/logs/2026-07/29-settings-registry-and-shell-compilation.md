## Donor Evidence

Loophole supplies a rich page set and host registry/state/set/reset protocol,
but its hardware, isolation, and keybinding pages retain specialist product
authority. Soundcheck mixes immediate preferences, discovery, product data,
integrations, and recovery in one dialog. Split-shell supplies the minimal
one-domain case and proves the shell cannot require optional workspace systems.

No donor proves a generic cross-domain atomic settings transaction. Longhorn's
implemented config store proves coordinated one-domain mutation, backup,
restore, profile transition, and exact receipts.

## Contract Decisions

- registry declarations seal into one deterministic host generation
- optional capability absence removes pages and empty sections
- one apply unit is the smallest failure-atomic authority
- the built-in config adapter binds one apply unit to one domain
- broader atomicity requires an explicit consumer transaction authority
- immediate and staged are timing modes
- activation is a separate successful-receipt result
- policy authority projects configured and effective values, provenance,
  constraints, and editability
- custom pages use renderer keys and standard session controllers
- settings search and links resolve stable metadata, never rendered DOM
- shared recovery pages retain contract-004 plans, confirmations, journals,
  rollback, and recovery gates
