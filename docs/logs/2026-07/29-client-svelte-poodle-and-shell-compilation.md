## Donor Evidence

The current clients prove checked protocol guards, listener-before-snapshot,
client epochs, and raw Tauri invoke/listen. They also duplicate lifecycle
mechanics and make transfer a mandatory dependency of the raw Tauri package.

Poodle publicly exposes the controlled layout primitives needed for tabs,
docks, splits, collapse, resize, snippets, eligibility, and same-window
movement.

Loophole's advanced drag behavior exposes what not to extract:

- generated tab-id parsing
- private Poodle class selectors
- component-private HTML5 payload knowledge
- capture-layer reconstruction of panel identity

The identical Loophole and Nucleus titlebar helpers remain a clean extraction
candidate once native drag and reporting are injected.

## Contract Decisions

- `@longhorn/core` owns structural transport and checked subscription lifetime
- domain packages retain validation and freshness comparison
- `@longhorn/tauri` has no mandatory domain dependency
- `@longhorn/svelte` root remains Surface-free
- optional domains use subpaths and optional peers
- private Poodle adapter proof uses one exact packable preview artifact
- Poodle needs a public pre-drag, lifecycle, eligibility, and external-drop
  seam before Longhorn drag bindings
- host sessions arm before native dragstart; late DataTransfer mutation is not
  allowed
- shell reuse is guidance and helpers, not one application frame
